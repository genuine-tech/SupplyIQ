02  —  Dual Risk Architecture

Transparent risk scoring. No black-box predictions.

Inventory Risk Engine: ML forecasts pure demand via Gradient Boosting regression (95.5% R²). A deterministic rule-layer computes lead-time demand, projected stock, and coverage days — decoupling prediction from automated decisions.

Supplier Delay Risk Engine: Heuristic composite scoring (0–100) combining delay rate (×0.4), normalized average delay (×0.3), and route risk factor (×0.3) — transparent and uncoupled from black-box assumptions.

  • CRITICALProjected stock < 0 & coverage < 3 days — immediate priority freight expedite
  • HIGHCoverage < 7 days — standard replenishment triggered within 48h
  • MEDIUMCoverage < 14 days — increased volume on next scheduled cycle
  • LOWCoverage ≥ 14 days — stock levels healthy, routine monitoring active
03  —  Global Transit Corridors
  1. Stage 01 · Preprocessing

    Data Ingestion & Cleaning

    10,997 daily records across 15 SKUs, 5 suppliers, and 3 warehouses. Pipeline performs deduplication, forward-fill imputation, label encoding, and IQR-based outlier audit before feature extraction.

  2. Stage 02 · Feature Engineering

    Temporal Feature Construction

    Automated lag features (1/3/7/14/28 days), rolling statistics (mean, std over 7/14/28 windows), calendar signals (day_of_week, is_weekend), and lead-time demand estimates fed into the model pipeline.

  3. Stage 03 · Training & Validation

    Chronological Split & Tuning

    Strict 70/15/15 chronological split prevents look-ahead leakage. RandomizedSearchCV with TimeSeriesSplit tunes RandomForest and GradientBoosting — champion model: GBR with MAE 17.56 (24% over 7-day MA baseline).

04  —  Autonomous Decision Engine

Real-time inference. Automated operational dispatch.

High-throughput FastAPI microservice serves continuous multi-horizon forecasts (1/7/14/30-day) with sub-50ms latency. A deterministic policy layer converts demand distributions into automated replenishment orders and priority freight expedite protocols when coverage breaches critical thresholds.

Interactive control center delivers 6 operational surfaces: Fleet Overview KPIs, Multi-Horizon Demand Curves, Stockout Risk Matrix, Supplier Reliability Scorecards, Correlation Analytics, and Holdout Diagnostics.
